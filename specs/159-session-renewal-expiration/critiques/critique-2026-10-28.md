## Executive Summary

The specification and plan are ready for task derivation after their stacked reviews. The product scope is grounded in
issue #220 and accepted session requirements, preserves the smallest service/store slice that delivers authoritative
renewal, and explicitly defers browser, comprehensive HTTP, recovery, and backend work. The engineering design extends
the existing public owner, keeps tenant policy validation at its current boundary, and uses one Redis linearization
point to prevent stale revival. No constitution conflict, product ambiguity, security gap, or architecture blocker was
found. Two implementation-time recommendations should be carried into tasks: validate the complete stored-record shape
before an atomic write and make both permitted Redis race orderings deterministically exercisable.
