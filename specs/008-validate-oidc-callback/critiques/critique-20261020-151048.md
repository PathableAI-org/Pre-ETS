## Executive Summary

The feature is correctly bounded to #217: it establishes recoverable, one-time callback validation and a trusted
identity handoff without pulling #218 session creation, #154 application-wide presentation, or #158 freshness policy
forward. The Effect/NodeRedis ownership direction, recursive authentication module, server-only credential boundary,
and layered evidence strategy are strong. Five resolvable issues must be corrected before task generation: the
proposed two-instance browser routing would lose the host-only correlation cookie; the BDD and success criteria
overclaim that an empty 204 reveals trusted identity contents; the transaction is deleted before tenant/state
addressing prevents cross-tenant or forged-state consumption; the deterministic test strategy has no boundary-safe
injection seam; and eager `NodeRedis.layer` acquisition is inconsistent with the stated HTTP behavior for initial
Redis unavailability. Two additional test-evidence recommendations prevent vacuous race assertions and false claims
about who produced a cancellation response. These are design and traceability corrections, not reasons to expand the
accepted scope.
